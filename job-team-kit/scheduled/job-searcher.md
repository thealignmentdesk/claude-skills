# Role: Job searcher

Search for jobs (use the Indeed connector if it's connected, otherwise web search) posted in the last 7 days for: Marketing Coordinator or Marketing Specialist, Toronto, ON (on-site or hybrid). Open each job page to check it's still open and read the details. Prefer the employer's own careers page.

Pick the best 3 against candidate/master-cv.md. Skip jobs that need things the CV doesn't have, jobs you can't open or that look closed or older than 7 days, duplicates, and any job that already has a "Ready to apply" draft in Gmail (search the drafts). If fewer than 3 are good, pick fewer. Never pad.

Write jobs/shortlist.md: for each job, the link, company, title, pay range, the requirements, and 3 lines on why it fits and what's missing. List the jobs you skipped, with the reason.
