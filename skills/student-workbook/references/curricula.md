# Official curriculum sources

Use the official source for the region. Quote expectations from it; never invent a code. Checked 13 Oct 2026. Curricula change, so if a link has moved, search the ministry or department site for the current document.

## Canada (curriculum is set by each province and territory)

**Ontario** (Ministry of Education, Curriculum and Resources): https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/
- Fastest: `python3 scripts/ontario_curriculum.py show <grade> <subject>` prints every expectation with code and official wording (grades 1-8 math, language, science; grade 9 math; `list <words>` finds other courses).
- Grades 1-8 math, Mathematics (2020): https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-mathematics/grades/g4-math (change `g4` to `g1`…`g8`).
  - Codes: strand letter, then overall expectation, then specific expectation. Grade 4 strands: A Social-Emotional Learning Skills and the Mathematical Processes, B Number, C Algebra, D Data, E Spatial Sense, F Financial Literacy. Example: **B1.4** "represent fractions from halves to tenths using drawings, tools, and standard fractional notation, and explain the meanings of the denominator and the numerator".
  - More grade 4 Number examples (official wording): **B1.2** "compare and order whole numbers up to and including 10 000, in various contexts"; **B1.3** "round whole numbers to the nearest ten, hundred, or thousand, in various contexts"; **B2.2** "recall and demonstrate multiplication facts for 1 × 1 to 10 × 10, and related division facts"; **B2.6** "represent and solve problems involving the division of two- or three-digit whole numbers by one-digit whole numbers, expressing any remainder as a fraction when appropriate, using appropriate tools, including arrays".
- Language (2023), grades 1-8: https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-language/grades/grade-4. Strands: A Literacy Connections and Applications, B Foundations of Language, C Comprehension, D Composition.
- Science and Technology (2022), grades 1-8: https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-sti/grades/grade-4. Strands: A STEM Skills and Connections, B Life Systems, C Matter and Energy, D Structures and Mechanisms, E Earth and Space Systems.
- Grade 9 math (de-streamed), MTH1W: https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-mathematics/courses/mth1w
- Grades 10-12: by course code. Pattern: subject letters + grade digit (1 = grade 9 … 4 = grade 12) + type (W de-streamed, D academic, P applied, U university, C college, M mixed, E workplace). Math examples: MCR3U (grade 11 functions), MHF4U (grade 12 advanced functions), MDM4U (data management). Ask which course the student takes.

**British Columbia:** https://curriculum.gov.bc.ca/ (Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, Content; no numbered codes. Quote the content item.)
**Alberta:** https://curriculum.learnalberta.ca/ (new K-6 curriculum being phased in; check which applies this year: https://www.alberta.ca/curriculum)
**Quebec** (Québec Education Program, PFEQ): https://www.quebec.ca/education/prescolaire-primaire-et-secondaire/programme-formation-ecole-quebecoise (French page; Quebec uses cycles: elementary cycle 1 = grades 1-2, cycle 2 = 3-4, cycle 3 = 5-6)
**Manitoba:** https://www.edu.gov.mb.ca/k12/cur/index.html
**Saskatchewan:** https://curriculum.gov.sk.ca/
**Nova Scotia:** https://curriculum.novascotia.ca/
**New Brunswick:** https://curriculum.nbed.ca/ (anglophone sector; francophone schools have their own programmes)
**Newfoundland and Labrador:** https://www.gov.nl.ca/education/k12/curriculum/
**Prince Edward Island:** https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/topic/curriculum

## United States (standards are set by each state)
- Many states use **Common Core** for math and English: official site https://corestandards.org/ (math PDF: https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Math_Standards1.pdf). Codes: grade.domain.cluster.standard, e.g. **4.NF.A.1**. (thecorestandards.org is an unofficial copy; fine for reading one standard, but cite the official site.)
- Several states have their own standards. Ask which state, then use that state's education department site. Examples: Texas TEKS https://tea.texas.gov/curriculum-and-instruction/texas-essential-knowledge-and-skills-teks ; Virginia SOL https://www.doe.virginia.gov/teaching-learning-assessment/k-12-standards-instruction
- Science: many states use NGSS (https://www.nextgenscience.org/).

## Other countries
- **England** (national curriculum, by key stage and year; no codes): https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-curriculum. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have their own curricula.
- **Australia** (Australian Curriculum v9): https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/ (content descriptions have codes; copy them from the site)
- **Nigeria** (NERDC): https://nerdc.gov.ng/
- **India** (NCERT syllabus; many states also have state boards): https://ncert.nic.in/syllabus.php
- Anywhere else: search for the national or state education ministry's curriculum page. If you can't find an official source, say so and name what you used instead.

## Grade words differ
"Grade 4" (Canada, US) = "Year 5" (England) = "Year 4" (Australia) = "Primary 4" (Nigeria) = "Class 4" (India). Confirm the school year the child is in, not just the age.
